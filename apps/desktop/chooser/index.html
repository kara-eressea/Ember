How should this app run?

Two ways to use it. You can change this later — Switch mode… in the menu.

Use locally

This computer runs the bouncer. Your characters stay connected to F-Chat for as long as the app is running, and your conversation history is stored here, on this machine.

Connect to my server

For a server you already run somewhere else. This window becomes a view onto it: that server holds your sessions and your history, so they stay online when this computer sleeps. You'll sign in with your account on that server.
