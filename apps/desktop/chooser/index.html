How should this app run?

Two ways to use it. You can change this later — Switch mode… in the menu.

Use locally

Everything runs on this computer — there's nothing to set up. Your characters stay connected to F-Chat while the app is running, even with the window closed, and your conversations are kept here on this machine.

Connect to my server

For people who already run this app on a server of their own. This window simply shows it: your characters stay connected and your conversations are kept over there, so nothing goes offline when this computer sleeps. You'll sign in with your account on that server.
